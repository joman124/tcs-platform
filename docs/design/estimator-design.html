On the patient copy

  • Service, provider, how often, per visit, total, estimated total, and the weekly, monthly and full-plan figures. Nothing else.
  • No CPT codes, add-on rows, plan or tier names, credentialing, warnings or buttons.
  • No disclaimer line (your call).
  • Rows never split across pages; the header repeats.

Admin-only

  • Status chips: ready, credentialing pending, blocked.
  • Blocked lines are left out of every total, and printing stays off until they are edited or removed.
  • Each row has Edit next to Remove. Edit reopens the add-a-line dialog titled "Edit service", prefilled from the line, with "Save changes"; saving replaces the line in place. A saved choice that is no longer offered starts empty with a note to choose it again.
  • Out-of-network plans offer a switch to cash.

Assumptions to confirm

  • The wordmark is text until the logo file arrives.
  • Brand type is Gill Sans; this page shows Cabin where Gill Sans is missing.
  • Monthly = weekly × 52 ÷ 12. One-time services count only in the full plan.
